The word list has been normalized to JSON and combined with bilingual teaching metadata.
NGSL-derived data and adaptations are distributed under the same license.
All 2,809 bundled example sentences, Chinese translations and bilingual collocations
are original model-authored NGSL Go B1-B2 offline cards, generated once at development
time and maintained in data/offline_batches/cards_*.json (CC BY-SA 4.0).
These are not Tatoeba sentences or quotations from a commercial dictionary.
Additional common meanings, usage patterns, and independently explained phrases
are original model-authored learning material in data/common_senses/senses_*.json
(CC BY-SA 4.0), including bilingual examples for each sense. This is a learner
resource rather than an exhaustive dictionary.
Earlier authored teaching notes and word-formation exercises remain in data/curated.json
and data/fce_enrichment.json. All original teaching additions are CC BY-SA 4.0.
AI-generated additions are learning aids and may contain errors; they are not quotations from a dictionary.
The NGSL authors do not endorse this application.

Fixed word audio: Kokoro v1.0 / Misaki, voices bf_emma (UK), af_heart (US). Apache 2.0; see KOKORO_LICENSE.txt. The Kokoro model and English frontend resources are bundled for offline synthesis (Apache 2.0). The checked source and file hashes are in data/kokoro_model.json.
UK phonetic transcriptions are generated with Misaki for the target part of speech.
US transcriptions: open-dict-data/ipa-dict en_US, based on cmudict-ipa and syllabify (MIT); see IPA_LICENSE.txt. https://github.com/open-dict-data/ipa-dict
A matching pronunciation variant is selected for the target part of speech; original upstream data is retained for reproducibility.
